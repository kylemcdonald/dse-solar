# Archived arrival logistics views

The shipping-weight and Fiji customs views were removed from production on
2026-09-03 after the project equipment arrived in Fiji. Their last components
and regression tests are retained here for audit history and possible reuse,
but nothing in the production viewer imports or links to them.

The underlying delivery, customs, and receipt-index data remains tracked. New
purchase records should continue to update those ledgers where applicable even
though these arrival-only screens are no longer shipped to users.

# DSE project rules

## Electrical protection policy

- Prefer resettable circuit breakers over field-replaceable fuses throughout the design. The site is a remote island where replacement fuses are not reliably available.
- Do not introduce an ATO/ATC, MIDI/AMI, Class T, gPV-link, or fused distribution block when a correctly rated breaker can provide the required protection and interrupt rating.
- Low-cost CHTAIXI branch breakers are acceptable only with correct DC polarity, conductor ampacity, fault-current suitability and installer approval. The selected high-current positions use three ordered non-polarized DIHOOL 120 A devices in DZ47X-125 frames; confirm the received DC interrupt rating, terminal capacity and torque. Every MidNite device and the superseded polarized CHTAIXI B125 devices remain uninstalled return/alternate items, not design components.
- Manufacturer-supplied/internal fuses that cannot be replaced without violating equipment instructions are exceptions, not templates for new distribution choices. Identify them explicitly and carry exact spares before travel.
- Treat nominal systems at 24 VDC or less as touch-safe for enclosure and partition decisions. This does not make battery fault current, arcs, hot conductors, terminal guarding, strain relief, torque, or overcurrent protection optional.
- The battery-adjacent cutoff enclosure contains exactly three purchased non-polarized DIHOOL 120 A devices: string A positive, string B positive and the SmartSolar battery-side cutoff. Do not place service breakers, buses, the SmartShunt, switches or converters in this box. Mount it as close to the battery positives as practical so both unprotected string-positive leads remain shortest-practical and guarded.
- R30 uses exactly four Victron BAT412201104 12 V / 220 Ah GEL Deep Cycle batteries as two independently breakered 24 V series strings in parallel: 24 V / 440 Ah, 10.56 kWh nominal and 5.28 kWh at the 50% planning depth of discharge. The batteries have M8 drilled flat-copper terminals. Use an 88 A DVCC ceiling with both strings healthy and 44 A after safely isolating one failed string, following Victron's preferred 0.2 C charge-current limit. Never equalize GEL batteries.
- MultiPlus-II AC-out-1 still requires downstream earth-leakage and overcurrent protection per Victron. Use one correctly selected Type A RCBO to provide both functions; do not remove it as “redundant” and do not add a second series breaker without a documented need.
- Keep the AC-input and AC-output protection assemblies together beside the MultiPlus, but do not use either purchased Mollom HT-8 as the AC box: the advertised 200 × 155 × 92 mm shell is too small for the canonical AC bend space, separation and bottom gland row. The second HT-8 is the accepted verified-fit enclosure for exactly the three battery/MPPT cutoff devices; retain the first as an AC-unsuitable spare. Treat input and output as two electrically independent L/N circuits: never combine their neutrals and never add a permanent N–PE link. PE is never switched. The current graph models a continuous generator → input protector → MultiPlus → output protector → socket PE chain through dedicated pass-through splices, with the main PE bond joined at the input PE point. Select the separate AC installation enclosure only after a 1:1 backplate, bend-radius and gland check. If any received terminal cannot accept its exact scheduled conductor count, use an accepted terminal block rather than crowding or doubling conductors.
- The two purchased DIHOOL B0DCN7HK57 devices are 30 A and remain safety-hold/return candidates. They cannot be the sole overcurrent protection for the planned 10 A Type I input lead or output socket. Two 10 A two-pole DIHOOL B0CRKNJSRH AC protection candidates are now purchased; keep the AC protection assembly on safety hold until the received devices are verified for 10 A overcurrent protection, 30 mA Type A residual-current protection, two-pole disconnection, interrupt rating, neutral-earth behavior, local acceptance and the exact surge-protection classification. Do not describe the integrated surge element as formally Type 2 unless the received marking or manufacturer documentation establishes that classification.
- R30 places exactly three branch breakers in the secondary-services enclosure: the purchased CHTAIXI B09H4W5HSW 10 A shared switched-services breaker and the two purchased 32 A CHTAIXI breakers for Orion input and the four-module ChargeIT positive feed. The shared branch is about 60 W expected / 100 W conservative and 5 A at the 20 V design floor, so 10 A is appropriate; use 160 W as the project expansion ceiling rather than treating 240 W at nominal voltage as a continuous allowance. Keep the B10 device on commissioning hold until its received 12–110 VDC, polarity, curve, 6 kA claim, terminal, torque, temperature and local-acceptance evidence is verified. Before energizing, coordinate the source-side protective device and secondary-feeder ampacity with both 100 A Blue Sea 2314 buses. Orion +OUT/common return still feeds socket A and daisy-chains to socket B; its 60 A short-circuit envelope is not adequate protection for the 30 A harness. The Orion-input and ChargeIT branches use consolidated 6 mm² positive/negative conductors. The ChargeIT branch retains explicit three-way joins so each device terminal receives one field lead; verify that each received Phoenix plug accepts the selected conductor or use an approved pigtail transition without trimming strands. Four modules draw up to 312 W from the manufacturer data, despite 300 W named output capacity.
- The secondary enclosure also contains one Blue Sea 2314 secondary positive bus, one Blue Sea 2314 secondary negative bus, the six-gang switch panel, the USB-C converter and Starlink power routing. The purchased nominal 302 × 302 × 178 mm ventilated ABS shell is the accepted verified-fit enclosure for these contents; preserve its modeled clearances, vent function, sealing and entries.
- The selected main positive/negative busbars are the purchased Joinfworld 250 A four-stud pair, wall-mounted outside the enclosures with their supplied red/black covers installed. The adjacent IP65 SmartShunt remains uncovered. Battery negatives must go to SmartShunt BATTERY MINUS and only SmartShunt SYSTEM MINUS may feed the main negative bus. Apply the real controlled operating envelope to that common return: one tool at or below 1,200 W, ORION REMOTE H off and the ChargeIT breaker open during heavy tool use, and the Ekrano DVCC charge-current limit at 88 A for the 440 Ah Victron GEL bank. This models about 72.9 A discharge against the 120 A 1/0 envelope; all installed loads at once could reach about 115 A, so the normal rating is conditional and must be tested. Available solar lowers net battery discharge but is deliberately not credited in this limit, so the tool remains within budget under cloud; the 1,470 W array maximum is a source rating, not an added load. Keep a genuine hold for fault/OCP coordination and received conductor, lug and termination evidence; never infer cable ampacity from the SmartShunt's 500 A body rating. The AMOMD 600 A pair is excluded return-history inventory and must not appear in the design, customs manifest or shipping treemap. Required main-positive lug stacking is approved for the accepted layout; document the installed conductor order and preserve specified torque, adequate thread engagement and supplied covers.
- The MultiPlus-II 24/3000 direct DC branch is a genuine design hold. Its exact Victron installation table requires a dedicated 300 A external DC protective device and at least 50 mm² copper for 0–5 m. Do not treat internal inverter protection or the two 120 A battery-string breakers as a substitute. Preserve the remote-site breaker-first policy, but select a resettable DC device only after its curve, interrupt capacity, conductor coordination, 250 A bus implications and installer acceptance are resolved.
- No insulated positive/negative bulkhead feed-through studs have been purchased. Until a pair is selected and electrically and mechanically verified, model and build the secondary feeder as one protected positive conductor and one negative conductor through ordinary strain-relieved glands; do not imply a stud purchase or installed stud interface.
- Actual retained imported 1/0 AWG inventory is five Shirbly red/black pairs—three 1.5 ft pairs and two 2 ft pairs—for ten individual cables and 17 conductor-feet. All three 1 ft iGreely pairs and the one 2 ft iGreely pair were returned; their $94.34 combined refund is recorded as excluded purchase/return history, and they must not be counted as usable inventory. Use a retained fixed assembly only where received length, conductor markings and 3/8 in / M10 lug geometry fit the final supported route; purchase the remaining correctly terminated red and black 1/0 AWG cable onsite in Fiji, including both MultiPlus feeds. Retailer copy is not sufficient evidence that the imported conductor itself is tinned or UL 1426 marine cable. Favor the shortest compatible assemblies between batteries, the cutoff box, SmartShunt and main buses.
- R30 uses exactly three AIKO-A490-MCE54Mw 490 W modules in one 3S string: 1,470 W, 104.1 Vmp, 123.3 V Voc at 25 °C and about 127.37 V modeled cold Voc at 10 °C. The single string carries 14.13 A Imp / 14.88 A Isc on one 4 mm² positive/negative home-run pair. Install exactly one purchased 20 A common-trip two-pole CHTAIXI breaker as the cutoff in the PV junction box; leave the second 20 A unit uninstalled as a labeled spare or return it. Do not model or install comb rails, the supplied fuse links, an active SPD or a redundant PV disconnect. The older 32 A two-pole breakers remain incompatible return items because they exceed the AIKO module's 25 A maximum series-fuse rating. Verify the installed breaker's exact markings, polarity diagram, common-trip behavior, cold-Voc margin, DC interrupt rating, torque and local acceptance before commissioning.

## Private order-receipt ingestion

1. Put new receipt PDFs in `private/to-process/`. Never commit `private/` or expose receipt addresses, payment details, phone numbers, or account data in logs or generated public files.
2. Extract only the order date/number, product description, ASIN, quantity, item price, promotion, shipping, tax, grand total, ETA, and installation disposition.
3. Validate every line-item subtotal and the receipt grand-total arithmetic before changing public data.
4. Reconcile each item into `data/dse-system.json`, `data/dse-delivery.json`, and, for imported physical goods, `data/dse-customs.json`. Mark purchased-but-incompatible items explicitly; never silently model them as installed.
5. Update a retailer-specific private PII-free aggregate such as `private/amazon-orders-through-YYYY-MM-DD.json` or `private/home-depot-orders-through-YYYY-MM-DD.json`; do not mix another retailer into the Amazon reconciliation. The Amazon aggregate's current scope is incremental orders after the initial 17 Aug equipment/light purchases.
6. Only after validation, move each PDF from `private/to-process/` into `private/` and rename it `retailer-YYYY-MM-DD-order-ORDER-ID-short-item-slug.pdf`.
7. Keep ingestion scripts idempotent: rerunning must update existing order numbers and accept already-renamed receipts without duplicating BOM rows or totals. Confirm `private/to-process/` is empty when finished.
8. If additional receipt PDFs appear in `private/to-process/` during an ingestion run, include and reconcile them before declaring the queue empty rather than leaving a partially processed batch.
9. Regenerate `data/dse-receipts.json` after ingestion. It is the committed, PII-free bridge between public customs invoice footnotes and ignored private PDFs. Filenames may be public; PDF contents and extracted PII may not. In public checkouts, preserve the committed manifest when private reconciliation inputs are absent.

## Canonical system model

- DSE/Fiji is the only supported project. Do not restore a PG/PNG selector, dataset, mode or copy branch.
- `app/dseTopology.ts` is the single electrical and physical source of truth. Build generators resolve it into checked, source-hashed artifacts for the detailed diagram and 3D model; browser code only validates and hydrates those artifacts. Do not add a second hand-maintained wiring diagram or physical-layout JSON, and do not restore browser-side routing.
- Every physical device is one graph device, every physical terminal is one conductor attached to a device face, and every wire/core is one graph connection. New hardware changes should normally be one device entry plus its connections.
- Ordinary enclosures auto-size from their DIN/backplate contents and crossing cable bundles. Received `verified-fixed` shells retain separately declared exact physical dimensions for rendering/inspection plus lattice-aligned solver envelopes for deterministic routing. Every enclosure has one evenly spaced bottom gland row. Do not add extra gland rows, position exceptions, open-door geometry or hand-routed cable patches.
- Enclosure padding, DIN gaps, backplate gaps and gland spacing come from each junction definition. Do not restore hidden minimum gaps in the layout engine. Keep routing-world bounds and camera framing derived from resolved device extents so moving hardware does not require router/camera edits.
- The voxel router pre-reserves fixed gland throats and only each terminal's minimal physical escape. It then routes thickest cables first and occupies each completed full approach/path before solving the next cable, so a future thin route cannot displace an earlier thick one. It penalizes distance from the wall, blocks routing volumes around unrelated devices and locks routed cable cells to their connection. There is no fallback route; an A* failure, cable-centerline conflict or unrelated device intersection is a release blocker.
- Breakers use one renderer; pole count controls width at 20 mm per way on the shared 10 mm geometry lattice. Device terminals must remain on the owning device surface and wires must meet them along the terminal direction.
- Keep device/conductor identification available through the fixed-size bottom-right hover tooltip and selection inspector. Do not restore floating labels, show/hide-label controls or scale notes.
- There are no separate overview or per-enclosure tabs. The full detailed diagram renders all four enclosures—battery/MPPT cutoff, secondary services, PV and AC—with dashed yellow outlines and automatically ordered input-left/output-right/storage-signal-bottom edge conductors; selecting one transitions into a full-page internal subpatch with glands exactly on its diagram boundary. The external main buses and SmartShunt are not enclosure contents. The 3D scene renders open-front/no-door enclosure groupings with the real internal devices mounted on the backplate.
- The wiring projection collapses intrinsically mated inside/outside wall-penetration ports to one continuous wire while preserving both physical routes in 3D. Cable breakouts, service splits and PE daisy splices render as bodyless fan/Y junctions, not rectangular devices. Conductor sides and order are declarative; never hand-place diagram ports or routes.
